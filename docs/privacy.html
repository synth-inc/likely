1. Who we are

Likely is a macOS menu bar app made by Synthetic Exploration, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Synth”, “we”, “us”). This policy covers the Likely app, its license and update services, and this website (synth-inc.github.io/likely). Our Terms of Service cover your use of the paid app and services.

Likely’s source code is public under the GPL-3.0 license at github.com/synth-inc/likely, so you can check everything here against the code.

2. What we collect, and what we don’t

We (Synth) receive only what we need to sell and license the app:

  • App and install ID. When Likely activates and when it checks your license, it sends our server the app’s identifier (“likely”) and a random ID created when you install it. The ID isn’t derived from your hardware, name or files.
  • Account email and license status. If you buy a license, our server keeps your account email address and whether your license is active, which plan it is, and when a subscription renews or ends.
  • Network information. Like any web server, ours (and GitHub, which hosts downloads and this site) sees your IP address when the app or your browser connects.

We don’t collect:

  • your files, file names or folder contents;
  • your searches, screenshots or search history;
  • what your AI provider sends back;
  • analytics, telemetry or crash reports. Likely has none;
  • your card details, which go to Stripe and never reach us.

We don’t sell personal information or use it for advertising.

4. AI providers

Likely doesn’t run an AI. When you type a search, Likely starts the coding agent you chose in Settings (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, OpenCode or Hermes) on your Mac. The agent runs under your own account with that provider and searches with Spotlight and read-only tools. Likely then copies the files it suggests into your Likely folder.

What the agent sends to its provider is governed by your agreement with that provider and its privacy terms, not by this policy. We don’t receive your searches or the provider’s answers. Likely reads only the file suggestions the agent prints, on your Mac.

You control what goes to the provider: screenshots and your name each have a switch in Settings, the agent only searches when you type, and you choose which tools to add.

5. Payment

Purchases go through Stripe Checkout, and subscriptions are managed in Stripe’s billing portal. Stripe processes your payment under its own privacy policy. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach us. We receive the result of the purchase so we can turn on your license.

6. Updates

Paid builds of Likely check for updates with Sparkle. The update feed is on our server and the download comes from GitHub releases, so both see your IP address and app version. Sparkle’s optional system-profile reporting is off, so no information about your Mac is sent with the check. You can turn off automatic checks in Settings. Builds you make yourself from source don’t check for updates.

7. Data on your Mac, and how to delete it

Likely keeps its working data on your Mac, where we can’t see it:

  • ~/Likely (or the folder you chose): copies of suggested files. Copies are cloned locally and cleared shortly after the picker closes; your originals are never changed.
  • ~/Library/Application Support/Likely: search history (History), your tools (tools.json), eval cases and results (evals), and temporary screenshots (run, removed when the picker closes).
  • ~/Library/Logs/Likely: Likely’s log, which may mention past searches.
  • Likely’s preferences (inc.synth.likely), which hold your settings, the install ID and your cached license status, and a Keychain item (inc.synth.likely.server) with a sign-in token for the license server.

To delete search history, use Settings → History. To delete everything, quit Likely and delete the folders above, then run defaults delete inc.synth.likely in Terminal and remove the Keychain item in Keychain Access. Your AI provider’s CLI may keep its own session logs; see its documentation.

8. Retention

We keep account and license records (install ID, account email, license status) for as long as your license is active, and after that only as long as we need to for legal, tax and accounting purposes, or to resolve disputes. Stripe keeps payment records under its own policy. Server logs that include IP addresses are kept only as long as our hosting providers retain them.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export the personal information we hold about you, or to object to how we use it. To make a request, including asking us to delete your account and license records, email contact@cloudless.so from the address you bought with, or include your install ID (in Likely’s preferences). We’ll answer within the time the law requires. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Data on your Mac is already under your control; you can delete it at any time as described above.

10. Children

Likely isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version here and update the effective date. If a change is material, for example if Likely starts sending something new off your Mac, we’ll say so in the app or the release notes before it takes effect.
